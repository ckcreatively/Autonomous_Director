# Autonomous Cinematic Camera Director

This project explores how AI vision and motion planning can be combined
to autonomously record a subject in different cinematic styles.

## Goal
Build a software-first system that:
- Detects and tracks a subject in real time
- Applies rule-based cinematic camera behaviors
- Simulates drone camera motion
- Eventually integrates with real drone hardware

## Current Stage
Week 1: Subject tracking and cinematic framing in video.

## Roadmap
- [ ] Person detection and tracking
- [ ] Cinematic style definitions
- [ ] Camera motion simulation
- [ ] Simulator integration
- [ ] Real drone testing

## Why This Matters
Solo creators and professionals need intelligent camera systems
that understand intent, not just movement.
